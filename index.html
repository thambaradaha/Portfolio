About

I’m a Manufacturing and Industrial Engineer (BSc Hons, University of Peradeniya, CGPA 3.50) working as a Mechanical Engineer on can and bottle filling lines at Ceylon Beverage International. My work sits where maintenance, reliability and data meet: FMEA-minded troubleshooting, structured preventive maintenance, SAP-based spares control, and statistical analysis to find where losses really come from.

I trained in lean routines at Ansell Lanka, built PLC automation prototypes with Omron components, and presented research on combining statistical root cause analysis with machine learning for defect reduction. Based in Galle, Sri Lanka. Fluent in English and Sinhala.
